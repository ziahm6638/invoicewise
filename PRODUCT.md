# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finance operators reviewing supplier invoices as they arrive. They need to find a record quickly, compare the source document with extracted values, and understand which automated checks can be trusted.

## Product Purpose

InvoiceWise turns emailed invoices into structured, judged data ready for downstream accounting systems. The dashboard proves that work by keeping the original document, extracted evidence, and judgment results together.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and candid. The interface should feel like a dependable finance tool, not a promotional analytics dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid vanity metrics, decorative finance imagery, generic card grids, hidden processing failures, and interfaces that make automation appear more certain than its evidence supports.

## Design Principles

- Put the source document beside the result whenever space allows.
- Make exceptions and uncertainty easier to spot than successful routine work.
- Keep lists scannable and move evidence into the selected invoice view.
- Prefer familiar controls and the existing application vocabulary.
- Explain empty, processing, and failure states in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible text labels, semantic status copy, and sufficient contrast. Never rely on colour alone to communicate invoice or judgment state.
